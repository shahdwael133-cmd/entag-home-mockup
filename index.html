How ordering works

You send the file

A 3D file, a PDF drawing or a sample. Several parts can go in one quote.

You get your quote within 24 hours

Counted from when you send your file. For a sample, we confirm the timing when the part arrives.

We make it through a vetted workshop

We match your part to the right partner workshop and manage the order for you.

We inspect every part ourselves

Every part comes to our Quality Control Center before delivery. For CNC and sheet metal with a drawing, you can add a CMM, first article or measurement report when you upload.

It reaches you, and you can track it

Follow the order in your account until it arrives.

Common questions

Do I need a 3D file?

No. Send a 3D file, a PDF drawing or a sample.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can order from 1 piece.

How long does it take?

You get your quote within 24 hours of sending your file. Lead times depend on the process: 3D printing from 2 business days, CNC machining and tube from 7.

Do you hold ISO certification?

Entag does not hold ISO certification. Every part is inspected at our Quality Control Center before delivery.

How are my drawings kept confidential?

All uploads are secure and confidential, and we keep strict NDAs with our network.

Can you deliver outside Egypt?

Yes. We ship globally.
